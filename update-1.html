New Structures

Three structure datapacks are on the way. They generate only in newly explored chunks, so adventure beyond the old edges.

CONFIRMED

Structory

Atmospheric structures with light lore told through villagers: ruins, towers, cottages, graveyards, settlements, boats and more.

Mangrove Ruins hide deep in swamps. Taiga Ruins wait in colder climates. Witches brew something mysterious in the Old Manor, and a rare Graveyard can trap those who wander too far.

CONFIRMED

Structory: Towers

Biome-themed towers that fit their surroundings.

  • Outposts
  • Firetowers
  • Ocean monument pillars
  • Nether towers
  • End towers
ADDED

Dungeons and Taverns

Vanilla-friendly structures built to blend in with the base game: environmental builds like fire watch towers and taverns, plus dungeons like the Badland Miner Outpost and the Illager Hideout.

Level up the cartographer inside a tavern to apprentice, then move them anywhere you like to keep leveling and unlock new treasure charts. Structure loot also hides unique enchantments, found in places like shrines and graves, and leveling a librarian inside certain structures unlocks an exclusive trade.

Vanilla Tweaks pack: confirmed too. Full list of tweaks coming soon.

Enchantments & Items

Some enchantments unlock today!

Confirmed items are coming for sure. Possible ones may or may not make the cut. All can be found via the enchanting table, loot chests or villager trades, except the crafted items noted below.

CONFIRMED

Smelting

Pickaxe enchantment that auto-smelts ores as you mine them. Works with Hammer and Magnetic.

CONFIRMED

Timber

Axe enchantment that fells connected logs in one swing. Vanilla axes and logs only. Works with Magnetic.

CONFIRMED

Hammer

Three levels. Mines multiple blocks at once, like a hammer. Works with Smelting and Magnetic.

CONFIRMED

Magnetic

Tools and weapons auto-pickup mined blocks and loot straight into your inventory.

CONFIRMED

XP Boost

Three levels. Earn extra XP while using your tools and weapons.

CONFIRMED

Enchantment Extractor

A craftable item that pulls enchantments off armor, tools and weapons so you can reuse them as enchanted books.

POSSIBLE

Agility

Leggings enchantment, two levels. Raises your step height so you walk up blocks without jumping.

POSSIBLE

Thunder Strike

Mace enchantment, three levels. Landing a charged attack calls down lightning.

POSSIBLE

Death Back Potion

Drink it to teleport to your last death location. Craftable, and stacks to 16.

POSSIBLE · INSANELY RARE

Soulbound

Your item returns to you when you die. If it joins the SMP, it will be insanely rare, so treat any Soulbound item like treasure.
